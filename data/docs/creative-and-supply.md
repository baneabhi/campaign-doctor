# Creative and supply

Educational summary written for the Campaign Doctor demo. It is not vendor documentation.

## Creative approval and delivery cliffs

A campaign can bid only with creatives that are approved and serving. If a creative is disapproved, expires, fails a technical check or reaches the end of its scheduled dates, bids can drop sharply within a day. In the daily metrics this shows up as a cliff: bids, wins and spend fall together while the win rate stays roughly the same. A gradual decline points to price or audience problems; a cliff points to a creative, tag or settings change on that date.

## Supply and price floors

Supply is the inventory available for the targeting. Publishers and exchanges set price floors, the minimum price they will accept. When floors rise or a supply path is blocked, the campaign sees fewer eligible auctions or a lower win rate at the same bid. Seasonal demand, such as a retail peak, raises clearing prices across the market without any change on the campaign side.

## Viewability and brand-safety filters

Viewability thresholds, brand-safety categories and blocklists remove inventory before bidding. Strict settings reduce bid volume. If a campaign under-delivers and its targeting is otherwise broad, review whether these filters are stricter than the brief requires.

## Creative sizes

Campaigns need creatives in the sizes the inventory offers. Missing a common size, for example a popular mobile banner, excludes a large slice of supply. Check the creative sizes against the placement types being targeted.
